# Ash's Victuals

An experimental food mod.

### How Should Mutations Work

- Four Deviation System
    - When at Mean: 50/50
    - When 1 Deviation: 75/25
    - When 2 Deviation: 90/10
    - When 3 Deviation: 99/1
- When a piece of food is mutated in one direction, the others should mutate in the other
    - When at Mean or 1 Deviation: Don't apply any modifiers
    - When at 2 Deviation: 77/25
    - When at 3 Deviation: 90/10
    - When at 4 Deviation: 99/1

### Environmental Factors

- Brightness or Light (Current or Above block)
